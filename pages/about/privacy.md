This page explains what information is collected when you visit this website, why it is collected and what choices you have.

## Who is responsible

This website is maintained by the people and organisations listed on this website. For questions about this privacy statement or about your personal data, please open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/ELIXIR-Belgium/elixir-toolkit-theme).

## Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or submit personal information to browse this website.

### Hosting

Like every web server, the server that hosts this website may record technical information about each request, such as your IP address, browser type and the page you requested. This information is used to deliver the website and to keep it secure.

### Web analytics

We use [Matomo](https://matomo.org/), an open source analytics platform, to understand how this website is used, for example which pages are visited most and how visitors find us. The data is processed on our own Matomo server and is not shared with third parties.

- **If you accept cookies**, Matomo uses cookies to recognise returning visits, which gives us more accurate statistics.
- **If you decline cookies or make no choice**, Matomo still counts visits, but without storing any cookies on your device. We cannot recognise you across visits.

We use [Google Analytics](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/) to understand how this website is used. Google Analytics is only loaded **after you accept cookies**. If you decline or make no choice, no data is sent to Google. Read [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. This website uses the following cookies:

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ett_cookie_consent` | Remembers whether you accepted or declined cookies | 1 year | After you make a choice |
| `_pk_id.*` | Matomo: distinguishes returning visitors | 13 months | Only after you accept |
| `_pk_ses.*` | Matomo: groups page views into a single visit | 30 minutes | Only after you accept |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics: distinguishes visitors and sessions | Up to 2 years | Only after you accept |

## Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its processing and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details above.

## Changes to this statement

We may update this statement when the website or the services it uses change. The latest version is always available on this page.
